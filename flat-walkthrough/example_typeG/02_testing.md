# Testing report — Unreal environment and packaged game

Engine: Unreal Engine 5.8 (installed build), macOS on Apple M5 Pro, Metal SM6. Lumen GI and reflections, virtual shadow maps.

## 1. Architecture vs. floor plan (Blender)

* An orthographic top view, cut at 2.0 m, was overlaid on `plan_my_flat.jpg` at the drawing's own scale (`previews/final/plan_overlay_walls.jpg`).
* Every wall, opening, duct and balcony coincides with the plan's wall lines to within a few centimetres.

## 2. Automated checks in the Unreal editor (`unreal/scripts/ue_verify.py`)

Results are in `unreal/verify/verify_report.json`. The player capsule is radius 25 cm × half-height 88 cm (shoulder width ≈ 50 cm, eye height ≈ 1.65 m), walk speed 3 m/s.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Walking routes: player-sized capsule swept from the entrance spawn into all 11 spaces (living, dining, kitchen, both balconies, wash alcove, both bedrooms, both toilets, common passage), through every doorway | **All 11 clear** (final run) |
| Floor-hole check: 1,500 downward traces on a 25 cm grid in 10 rooms | **0 misses** |
| Assets: 332 meshes, 74 materials, 87 textures imported; 346 actors and 40 lights placed | OK |
| Packaging: Shipping cook, 1,069 packages | `BUILD SUCCESSFUL`, 0 cook errors |

**Issues the earlier runs found, and what fixed them:**
* **L-shaped kitchen counter and cabinets.** They had bounding-box collision that blocked the whole kitchen. Furniture now uses per-polygon collision.
* **Toilet 2 door.** Open at 90° it stood across the 150 cm-wide room and blocked the WC and shower. It is now hinged on the lower jamb so it rests against the wall. The swing on the drawings is ambiguous, so this is an assumption.
* **Bedroom 2 door.** As drawn, its swing overlaps the toilet 2 doorway. It now stands fully open against the toilet wall and its leaf has no collision, so it can't trap the player.
* **Desk chairs in both bedrooms.** They blocked the ~75 cm passage beside the beds. They are now tucked under the desks.
* **Balcony 2 bistro set.** It filled the whole 1.47 m depth. It was replaced by two tub chairs and a side table at the kitchen end. The balcony 1 side table was also moved.
* **Toilet doors at 80–85°.** They narrowed the 75 cm doorways. They now open to 90°.
* **Living armchair.** Moved 18 cm to widen the lane between it and the TV unit to about 85 cm.

## 3. Packaged game (`build/Mac/FlatTypeG.app`)

* **Launch:** the app starts, loads `L_FlatTypeG` and renders with materials, Lumen lighting and the sky. See `previews/game/`.
* **Movement and collision:** walking with WASD moved the player from the entrance, through the living room, into the foyer without passing through walls or furniture. Mouse-look works.
* **First-person arms:** hidden, so the view is a clean walkthrough.
* **Mouse sensitivity (rebuild of 26 Oct):** halved with a 0.5 Scalar modifier on `IA_MouseLook` (`unreal/scripts/ue_mouse_sensitivity.py`). The value was confirmed after reloading the asset in a fresh editor process, and the build was a full recook. The repackaged app launches and renders, and the view responds to the mouse. The exact turn rate was not measured, because synthetic mouse input doesn't reach the game once it captures the cursor.
* **Packaging fix:** UE 5.8's `-archive` step copied an incomplete bundle without paks or engine content, which crashed at launch. The deliverable is the complete staged bundle instead. `unreal/scripts/package_mac.sh` does this automatically.

## 4. Not verified, or limitations

* **Navmesh:** path queries ran but did not give conclusive results, because the navigation build was locked during the automated session. Accessibility is established by the capsule sweeps, which use the real player collision. The game itself does not use a navmesh.
* **Frame rate:** not measured with counters, since Shipping builds have no stat overlay. Movement in the packaged build looked smooth at 1280×720 windowed on the M5 Pro. Untested on other or lower-end Macs.
* **Doors are fixed open.** There is no open/close interaction, because the project is Blueprint-only and generated by script. The bedroom 2 door leaf can be walked through, by design (§2).
* **Not tested:** Windows, gamepad and touch input.
